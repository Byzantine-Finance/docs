---
title: "Required documents"
---

Depending on whether the user is an individual or a business entity, different documents are required for KYC and KYB.

We accept the following documents:

## For individuals:

### ID documents

- Passport (`passport`)
- National ID card, front and back (`national_id_front`, `national_id_back`)
- Driver's licence, front and back (`drivers_license_front`, `drivers_license_back`)
- Residence permit, front and back (`residence_permit_front`, `residence_permit_back`)

<Info>
  A passport or national ID card is preferred: it is accepted in every country supported by Bridge. Send the `expirationDate` of the document: expired documents are refused, and fiat endorsements are revoked when the document on file expires.
</Info>

### Proof of address

<Info>
  All proof of address documents must be issued within the last 90 days. Send the document's `issueDate`.
</Info>

Upload it with the `proof_of_address` document type. Accepted documents include:

- Utility bills
- Recent bank statements
- Mobile phone bills
- Tax documentation

### Signatory authority

A signer of an entity who is neither director, owner nor representative must provide a letter of authority with the `proof_of_signatory_authority` document type. It is stored with the entity's documents.

<Note>
  `proof_of_source_of_funds`, `proof_of_source_of_wealth` and `other` are not accepted yet for individuals and associated persons: requests containing them are refused.
</Note>

## For legal entities:

Submit at least one document from each of the four required groups below. The `missingDocuments` field uses the code shown beside each heading until that group is covered. It can also contain questionnaire-backed requirements for the entity's physical address, business activity, financial information, or high-risk information; see the [KYB guide](/api-reference/kyb#2-complete-the-company-information).

### Company details (`COMPANY_DETAILS_DOCUMENT`)

- Certificate of incorporation (`incorporation_cert`)
- Articles of association (`incorporation_articles`)
- State or company registry extract (`state_registry`), including an attestation d’immatriculation au RNE or Kbis in France

### Registry extract for EEA businesses (`REGISTRY_EXTRACT_DOCUMENT`)

Businesses incorporated in the EEA must provide both a formation document (`incorporation_cert` or `incorporation_articles`) and a recent registry extract (`state_registry` or `good_standing_cert`).

### Ownership structure (`OWNERSHIP_STRUCTURE_DOCUMENT`)

- Shareholder registry (`shareholder_registry`)
- Trust agreement (`trust_agreement`)
- Partnership agreement (`partnership_agreement`)
- Statement of information (`information_statement`)

### Source of funds (`SOURCE_OF_FUNDS_DOCUMENT`)

Upload the supporting file with the `source_of_funds` document type. Common examples include:

- audited financial statements;
- bank or brokerage account statements;
- tax returns;
- invoices to clients proving the nature of the business.

### Proof of business address (`PROOF_OF_ADDRESS_DOCUMENT`)

The document must be in the entity's name, prove its physical address, and be issued within the last 90 days. Upload it with the `proof_of_address` document type. Common examples include:

- utility bills;
- lease agreements;
- bank statements;
- government or tax correspondence.

### Conditional documents

These documents are required only when the entity's answers call for them. None of them satisfies one of the four required groups above.

| Document type | Code in `missingDocuments` | Required when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `proof_of_nature_of_business` - invoices, contracts or service agreements showing the business performs the activities it declares | `PROOF_OF_NATURE_OF_BUSINESS_DOCUMENT` | The business has no website. |
| `ownership_chart` - diagram of direct and indirect ownership adding up to 100% | `OWNERSHIP_CHART_DOCUMENT` | An intermediate legal entity owns 25% or more of the business. |
| `flow_of_funds` - movement of money through the business and the account | `FLOW_OF_FUNDS_DOCUMENT` | Money services are conducted through the account. |
| `regulatory_license` - proof of registration with the regulator | `REGULATORY_LICENSE_DOCUMENT` | The business declares a regulated activity. |

### Representatives authorisation

<Info>
  Needed only in case a representative is not listed in the ownership structure or registration documents.
</Info>

- Power of attorney (`power_of_attorney`)

### Optional documents

Other entity document types include `directors_registry`, `good_standing_cert` (outside the EEA rule above), `incumbency_cert`, `source_of_wealth`, `aml_policy`, `aml_comfort_letter`, `proof_of_entity_name_change`, and `other`. They can provide supporting evidence but do not satisfy one of the required groups above.

Send the `issueDate` of each document, and its `expirationDate` when it has one: a registry extract or proof of address must be recent.
