---
title: "Required documents"
---

Depending on whether the user is an individual or a business entity, different documents are required for KYC and KYB.

We accept the following documents:

## For individuals:

### ID documents

- Passport
- National ID card (back and front side)

### Proof of address

<Info>
  All proof of address documents must be issued no more than 3 months ago.
</Info>

- Utility bills
- Recent bank statements
- Mobile phone bills
- Tax documentation

## For legal entities:

Submit at least one document from each of the four required groups below. The `missingDocuments` field uses the code shown beside each heading until that group is covered. It can also contain questionnaire-backed requirements for the entity's physical address, business activity, or financial information; see the [KYB guide](/api-reference/kyb#2-complete-the-company-information).

### Company details (`COMPANY_DETAILS_DOCUMENT`)

- Certificate of incorporation (`incorporation_cert`)
- Articles of association (`incorporation_articles`)
- State or company registry extract (`state_registry`), including an attestation d’immatriculation au RNE or Kbis in France

### Ownership structure (`OWNERSHIP_STRUCTURE_DOCUMENT`)

- Shareholder registry (`shareholder_registry`)
- Trust agreement (`trust_agreement`)
- Partnership agreement (`partnership_agreement`)
- Statement of information (`information_statement`)

### Source of funds (`SOURCE_OF_FUNDS_DOCUMENT`)

Upload the supporting file with the `source_of_funds` document type. Common examples include:

- audited financial statements;
- bank or brokerage account statements;
- tax returns;
- invoices to clients proving the nature of the business.

### Proof of business address (`PROOF_OF_ADDRESS_DOCUMENT`)

The document must be in the entity's name and prove its physical address. Upload it with the `proof_of_address` document type. Common examples include:

- utility bills;
- lease agreements;
- bank statements;
- government or tax correspondence.

### Representatives authorisation

<Info>
  Needed only in case a representative is not listed in the ownership structure or registration documents.
</Info>

- Power of attorney (`power_of_attorney`)

Other optional entity document types include `directors_registry`, `good_standing_cert`, `incumbency_cert`, and `other`. They can provide supporting evidence but do not satisfy one of the four required groups above.
