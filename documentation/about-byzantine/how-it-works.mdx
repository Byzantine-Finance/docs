# Four steps. Full visibility.

## 1. Add EUR or USD

Send funds by bank transfer. Eligible self-managed setups can also use a supported direct transfer.

## 2. Move funds into the strategy

Regulated partners handle any required conversion. Funds then move to the venues approved for the selected strategy.

## 3. Put the strategy to work

Keyrock Curation Services sets and monitors allocations within predefined parameters. Byzantine provides the interface and technical infrastructure.

## 4. Track and withdraw

Follow balances, returns and activity from your dashboard. Request a withdrawal at any time, subject to liquidity and partner processing.

## Who does what

- **Byzantine:** Interface and technical infrastructure
- **Keyrock Curation Services:** Strategy curation and monitoring
- **Third-party providers:** Regulated services such as conversion and custody
- **You:** Authorise activity and submit withdrawal instructions

Byzantine does not hold client assets or carry out regulated activities.
