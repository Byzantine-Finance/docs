# Know what happens next

| Transfer | Typical timing |
| --- | --- |
| **EUR - Instant SEPA** | Usually under 30 seconds |
| **EUR - Standard SEPA** | 1-3 business days |
| **USD - Same-day ACH** | Same business day when sent before the applicable cutoff |
| **USD - Standard ACH** | 1-3 business days |
| **USD - Wire** | Typically within 2 hours before the cutoff, otherwise the next business day |
| **Supported direct transfer** | Usually under 30 seconds |

For bank withdrawals, allow the same payment-rail timing after funds are released from the strategy.

Timelines are estimates and can vary by bank, payment partner, market liquidity and compliance checks.
