# Move money in. Move money out.

Add EUR or USD by bank transfer. A regulated payment partner handles any required conversion before funds are allocated through the selected strategy.

## Deposits

1. Open the deposit instructions in your dashboard
2. Send funds from a verified bank
3. Track the transfer through to allocation

## Withdrawals

Request a withdrawal at any time. Settlement timing depends on the currency, payment rail, bank, market liquidity and partner processing.

Only Admin users can submit withdrawals. View-only users can monitor balances and activity.
