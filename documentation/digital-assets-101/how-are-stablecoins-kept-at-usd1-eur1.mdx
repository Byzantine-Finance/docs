# One unit. One matching reserve.

The EUR- and USD-linked units used by Byzantine are issued by Circle and designed to be redeemable at equal value.

For each unit in circulation, the issuer holds matching reserves in cash and short-term government securities.

## How alignment works

1. When a unit is issued, the matching reserve is added
2. When a unit is redeemed, the currency is returned and the unit is removed
3. Independent attestations regularly report the level of reserves

<Note>
  Alignment depends on the issuer, reserve quality, redemption conditions and market conditions.
</Note>
