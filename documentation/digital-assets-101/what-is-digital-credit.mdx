# Borrower demand creates the return

Specialist borrowers provide collateral, access short-term EUR- or USD-linked liquidity and pay interest. That interest creates the return.

## How it works

1. Funds are made available through supported venues
2. Borrowers provide collateral worth more than the amount requested
3. The rate moves with supply and demand
4. Borrower interest contributes to the strategy return
