# Clear European rules

MiCA sets common European Union requirements for issuers of currency-linked units and certain service providers.

## What the rules cover

- Reserve composition and monitoring
- Issuance and redemption conditions
- Governance and transparency
- Permissions and supervision for relevant providers

Byzantine remains a technical infrastructure provider. Regulated services are provided by third parties under their own permissions and responsibilities.
