# Add another layer of protection

Optional coverage is available for specified technical and operational events. It sits alongside existing controls, including independent reviews, collateral requirements and continuous monitoring.

## What coverage may include

- Code failure or a technical vulnerability
- An external attack on the infrastructure
- Internal, external or transfer-related fraud

## What it does not cover

- Market movements
- Periods of reduced liquidity
- Compromise of your own access methods in a self-custody setup

Covered options are identified in the dashboard. The cost is already reflected in the displayed return.

<Note>
  Coverage is subject to the terms, limits and exclusions of the applicable policy. It does not guarantee full recovery.
</Note>
