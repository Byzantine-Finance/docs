## Keyrock

Keyrock is our partner acting as strategy curator. Keyrock curation consists of risk scoring and accepting assets in the vault. The risk models enable low risk high quality yield for depositors. It has no direct relationship with depositors and acts as a service provider to Byzantine for all operational purposes.

## Byzantine Finance

Byzantine Finance serves exclusively as technology provider linking partner systems together.

**Important:** User assets are never touched by Byzantine itself.
