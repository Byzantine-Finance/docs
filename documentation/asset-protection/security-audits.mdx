# Independent review, before launch

The contracts and adapters developed for Byzantine have been reviewed by eight independent security teams. Critical and high-severity findings identified during testing were remediated and checked again before launch.

## What is reviewed

- Access rights and authorisations
- Calculations, allocation rules and withdrawal requests
- Interactions with external services
- Known code exploitation scenarios
- Change and deployment processes

Audit reports are available for review.

Keyrock Curation Services also maintains a SOC 2 Type II report covering its operational controls. The issuer of the supported EUR- and USD-linked units publishes regular reserve attestations.
