# Clear roles. Clear responsibilities.

Byzantine provides the interface and technical infrastructure. Byzantine does not hold client assets, manage allocations or carry out regulated activities.

## Who is involved

- **Keyrock Curation Services:** Sets and monitors the strategy under its own status
- **Payment partners:** Handle transfers and any required conversion
- **Custody providers:** Provide supported custody arrangements
- **You:** Authorise activity and submit withdrawal requests

Each provider operates under its own permissions, processes and responsibilities.

Financial advisers can share factual information and introduce eligible clients. They do not sell Byzantine or make recommendations on Byzantine's behalf.
