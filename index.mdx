## One place. More control.

- **Access return opportunities.** Access returns of up to [7% APY\*](#performance-information), net of fees, depending on market conditions.
- **Access funds when you need them.** Request withdrawals at any time.
- **Stay in control.** Assets are held through supported custody or self-custody arrangements. Byzantine does not hold or move client assets.
- **See everything clearly.** Track your balance, performance, and activity from one simple dashboard.
- **Rely on specialist partners.** Strategy is curated by Keyrock Curation Services.
- **Add optional insurance.**
